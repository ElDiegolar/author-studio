Features

Your book, as a living story bible.

Premise, voice, world, cast, scene cards, a versioned manuscript and a continuity ledger, all connected. Every tool reads from it, and nothing changes without your say.

  • Characters are souls with a knowledge horizon

    Talk to any character as of any scene. They answer only from what they have lived up to that point. A deterministic leak detector flags any answer that mentions something they cannot know yet, so a chat never spoils your own plot.

    Ch. 1Ch. 3Ch. 5Ch. 7Ch. 9Ch. 12
    Leak flagged: “the fire at the archive” happens in Ch. 9, after Mara’s horizon.

  • A psychological mirror for each character

    See what drives a character, what they fear and where they contradict themselves, drawn from what is on the page.

  • Characters can decide for themselves

    Turn on agency per character and let them choose what to do next. Their choices stay scratch until you keep them.

  • Drafts in your voice, edits as diffs

    Draft from scene cards in your own voice. Every AI edit is shown as a diff, and nothing lands until you accept it.

  • A muse for writer’s block

    Stuck? Ask for ideas grounded in your characters’ souls: what each of them would want, fear or try next.

  • Proofread, line edit, verify

    Proofreading and line editing, plus deterministic checks for continuity contradictions, timeline, POV, tense and length. Read the whole book page by page, then export it to Word, EPUB, PDF, Markdown or plain text.

  • Bring the manuscript you already have

    Import Word, Markdown, plain text, RTF, OpenDocument, EPUB, HTML, PDF, Final Draft or Scrivener. Author Studio splits it into chapters and scenes and, with AI on, builds the characters, world and scene cards for you.

  • A studio agent, and an MCP server

    Do everything by chatting with the studio agent. An MCP server lets other AI agents work with your book too.

Local-first

Your book never leaves your machine.

Author Studio is a desktop app, not a website. Your manuscript, notes and characters are saved as ordinary files in your Documents folder. Nothing is uploaded.

  • Files you own

    Plain JSON and Markdown in Documents\Author Studio. Back them up, sync them, or open them in any editor.

  • Works offline

    Use a local model for fully offline AI, or turn AI off and write by hand.

  • Bring your own AI

    Use your own Anthropic API key or Claude Code if you have it. Requests go straight from your machine to the provider you chose.

  • No training on your words

    Author Studio never uploads your writing and never trains a model on it.

Writer soul

It learns your voice. You decide what counts.

The writer soul studies your own prose and every AI draft you rewrite. It proposes style rules with before-and-after evidence, and you mark each one “This is me” or “Not me”.

  • Habits measured from your edits, not guessed from a questionnaire.
  • Style-drift check tells you when a chapter stops sounding like you.
  • Follows you across books, and a series can pin book one’s voice.

FAQ

Honest answers.

Is my writing uploaded anywhere?

No. Your book is stored on your own computer as plain JSON and Markdown files in Documents\Author Studio. Nothing is uploaded, and no model is trained on your writing. If you choose a cloud AI provider, only the requests you make are sent to that provider, using your own key.

Do I need AI to use Author Studio?

No. AI is optional. You can write by hand and still use the story bible, scene cards, versioned manuscript, deterministic checks for continuity, timeline, POV and tense, the page-by-page reader, and export to Word, EPUB, PDF and Markdown.

Which AI can I use?

Your own Anthropic API key, Claude Code if it is installed on your computer, or a local model that runs fully offline. You can also turn AI off entirely.

What files can I import?

Word, Markdown, plain text, RTF, OpenDocument, EPUB, HTML, PDF, Final Draft and Scrivener. Your manuscript is split into chapters and scenes, and when AI is on, characters, world entries and scene cards are built automatically.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Writing and the deterministic checks work without a connection, and with a local model the AI features run fully offline too.

Will it write my book for me?

No. It proposes and you decide. Every AI edit is shown as a diff that you accept or reject, character decisions stay scratch until you keep them, and style rules only count once you mark them as yours.

Which platforms are supported?

Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) today. A macOS version built from the same code is coming soon, and an iOS version is planned.
